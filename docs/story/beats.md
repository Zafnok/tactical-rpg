# Nick's story beats — CANON

Decided: 2026-09-25
Source: ticket 0007

Everything below is Nick's own words, verbatim. **This file is canon.** Story
work (0701 and later) must not contradict it. Nothing has been added: where
Nick gave no beat, there is no beat. Claude's interpretations live in
[`docs/design/setting-and-tone.md`](../design/setting-and-tone.md), not here.

## Setting

> "1A to start, with potential for additional continents that might feel
> different (i.e. start in Europe-like countries / army compositions, travel to
> Asia and encounter wuxia inspired encounters... etc)"

(1A = classic medieval fantasy war, Fire Emblem-style.)

## Tone

> "2B"

(2B = dark with warmth and humour, like FE Path of Radiance / Triangle Strategy.)

## Who the player follows

> "3A to start. It would be nice to aim for C eventually but let's get one lord
> down solidly before expanding scope. But this doesn't mean the supporting
> cast should be cast aside, they should also have some arcs or role to play
> in the story etc as well as support conversations"

(3A = a single lord; C = ensemble with rotating viewpoints.)

## The lord

**Who they are at the start:**

> "L1 C - we can expand more on what exact inciting incident happened"

(C = a disgraced or exiled noble.)

**Personality:**

> "L2 frankly I am a bit tired of B in game writing and C seems it would get
> annoying after a long playthrough so I'm leaning between A and D. D would be
> harder for sure but at the same time Joker is still a really cool guy so
> there can still be charm there. I'm worried if we write the character one
> way, and there's no character choice (i.e. three houses, fortune's weave)
> then the player will get tired of one personality. Like for me I'm playing
> Leda and she's fucking crazy I'm like what?? she's just a murderer?"

**Age and gender:**

> "L3 I'm thinking 18-25. If we go with D for L2 then player can choose. If we
> go with L2 A then fixed gender - probably male because I do not want to even
> attempt to give feedback on female writing for a lead... again I always
> write them wrong and even with AI assistance I don't trust myself. But the
> supporting cast can of course have written females we'll just be careful."

**Final shape:**

> "B"

(B = Persona-style lead: defined background and look, few spoken lines,
player-picked reply tones at key moments that do not branch the plot; the
player chooses the lead's gender.)

## Beats

**Inciting incident:**

> "I think we need to anchor on the main character first, figure out a persona
> for them before suddenly putting together a big bang event. No commitment
> here yet"

**Twist / midpoint:**

> "Same as 1"

**Ending:**

> "The very end of the game should not be a tragedy. It should feel resolved
> and like a hardfought victory. There might have been some unavoidable losses
> (i.e. story causes you to lose someone close, think expedition 33 after act 1)"

**Characters he already imagines:**

> "Let's really drive this home with more leading questions"

**Off-limits:**

> "Let's avoid romance. Simply, I do not trust myself not to get horny if we
> start writing romance flags etc and then it would derail the core gameplay.
> No romance whatsoever, or if there is any, it should be pre-established not
> built up over time. Avoid shipping."

**Stories he likes:**

> "Some stories I liked are Expedition 33, Persona 5 Royal, Persona 3 Reload,
> Cyberpunk 2077, Disco Elysium, Nier Automata, Yakuza series, Witcher 3,
> Resident Evil, I like Dark Souls lore, Baldurs Gate 3 choices. These are just
> my preferences they are not a permission to take every story beat from any of
> them but if you can find a common pattern amongst these and use that as
> inspiration OK"
