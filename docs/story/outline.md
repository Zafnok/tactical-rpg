# Story outline

Status: **draft for gate 2** (ticket 0701). Nick's feedback goes at the
bottom.

Built from [`beats.md`](beats.md) (canon), [`bible.md`](bible.md), the
[character sheets](characters/) and `docs/design/world-structure.md`. Names are
placeholders ([registry](names.md)).

Rules this outline follows:

- A chapter is a **story beat**, not a battle (`chapter-1.md`). Chapters 1–3
  are **linear**, one battle each. From Chapter 4, each chapter plays out on the
  act's **world map** and ends with its **story battle**
  (`world-structure.md`).
- **Every chapter advances the main plot and at least one personal arc.**
- **No branching routes.** Reply tones never change what happens.
- **Each continent is its own act**, with its own world map, and the player
  can't go back to an earlier act's map.
- Levels, enemy counts, map sizes and turn counts are for the chapter tickets.
  "Map idea" here is only a pitch.

## The shape of the game

| Act | Continent | Chapters (rough) | What it's about |
| --- | --------- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| **1. The Exile** | The Old Kingdoms: Ardeval | 1–8 | The lead breaks exile, learns the truth about Dace and the Door, loses Hollis, and flees overseas as a traitor. |
| **2. The Far Shore** | The Jade Reach (east, wuxia-inspired) | about 9–16 | Strangers in a land that has seen the Door open before. Learning how it was shut. **The midpoint twist.** |
| **3. The Door** | The Old Kingdoms at war (a new map of Ardeval and Brennmark) | about 17–24 | Home, to end the war and shut the Door. Dace turns. The hard-fought victory. |

Continent changes: Act 1 → 2 (west to east) and Act 2 → 3 (east to home).
Act 3's home continent is a **new map**: the same land, but at war, with new
nodes and some old places burned. The finale revisiting home is the story
choice `world-structure.md` leaves to this outline.

**Proposal for gate 2: Chapter 3 is linear**, so the world map opens at
Chapter 4, one chapter later than the default in `world-structure.md`. Reason:
Chapters 1–3 are one tight story run (the killers, the false flag, the
register) that turns the lead from exile into outlaw, and gives the player a
clear goal (Dace, at Veyne Hall) before the map opens.

## Act 1: The Exile (the Old Kingdoms: Ardeval)

### Chapter 1: The Letter (linear)

- **Goal:** survive. The Red Company has come to Harrowby to kill the lead,
  Hollis and "the girl with burned hands".
- **Conflict:** Red Harl's Unpaid band holds the old toll-fort by the ford, and
  the lead has no army, only five people who happen to be there.
- **Turn:** Harl's orders carry the **Veyne seal**, which only Dace holds.
  Rue has seen it before, on Vosse's cart. Dace sent the warning *and* the
  killers.
- **Map idea:** the `chapter-1.md` sketch. The hamlet at the bottom left, the
  river with a bridge and a road, a forest lane, and Harl on the fort at the
  top right. Rout.
- **Arcs advanced:** Hollis (his son's hand, then his son's seal), Tamsin
  (kills her old captain), Piers (his report brought them), Rue (the seal).
- **Recruits:** the Chapter 1 six: the lead, Hollis, Tamsin, Aske, Piers and
  Rue.
- Beat sheet: [`chapters/ch01.md`](chapters/ch01.md).

### Chapter 2: Kell's Ford (linear)

- **Goal:** leave the Thornmarch by its only bridge, at Kell's Ford, and head
  for Veyne Hall to ask Dace to his face.
- **Conflict:** Kell's Ford is burning. "Brennish raiders" are killing
  villagers while the Ardevali garrison across the river does nothing.
- **Turn:** Aske sees it first: "These are not Brennish. Brennish cannot
  afford boots like this." The raiders are **Hounds in Brennish colours**.
  After the battle a crown herald proclaims that Brennmark has broken the Ash
  Peace, and names the lead an outlaw "seen riding with the raiders". The war
  has started, and it's a lie.
- **Map idea:** a river village split by a ford. Burning houses to reach
  (villages), the garrison's watchtower across the water, and a Hound
  lieutenant as boss. Objective: defeat the boss (the chapter ticket
  decides).
- **Arcs advanced:** Aske (his hatred loses its target), Piers (healing
  strangers under fire), the lead and Dace (Dace's Hounds did this).
- **Recruit:** **Joss Pellam** (Swordsman), a young Hound who won't kill
  villagers. Talk-recruit with the lead or Aske.

### Chapter 3: Greywater (linear)

- **Goal:** find out why the Vigil wanted Rue. Piers knows that every gifted
  child's name, and where they were sent, is written in the register at
  Greywater Abbey.
- **Conflict:** the abbey is on a lake island. Its prior is burning records
  under Hound guard, and taking the register makes Piers an apostate.
- **Turn:** the register holds:
  - Rue's name, twice: once for Vosse's cart, once as a Candle;
  - Wren's name: "Ashfields: lost";
  - Vosse's pay-book: "D. Marr, informer: eleven children".

  Rue tells the lead what she saw at the mill, and realises the lead didn't
  know. Piers confesses to Rue that his report brought Harl. Hollis says
  nothing, which Rue notices.
- **Map idea:** a causeway to an island abbey, with wardens and Hounds on the
  walls. The scriptorium is the Seize point. The reliquary has chests.
- **Arcs advanced:** Piers (breaks with the Vigil), Rue (tells), Hollis (his
  silence starts to show), the lead and Dace (the truth, on paper).
- **Recruits:** none.
- **After this chapter the Act 1 world map opens.**

### Chapter 4: The Hound's Country (world map opens)

- **Goal:** reach Veyne Hall and confront Dace.
- **Conflict:** the roads of southern Ardeval are garrisoned by Hounds, and the
  lead is a named outlaw.
- **Turn:** Dace doesn't deny it: "I did what fed us. You had a house; I had
  a sister." Then Dace says, to Hollis: "You knew. You always knew." The lead
  learns Hollis kept the secret for four years. Dace escapes.
- **Story battle: "Veyne Hall".** Storming the lead's own childhood home.
  Hollis and the lead know the old servants' passages. Dace is the boss and
  escapes at low HP.
- **Fixed skirmishes:**
  - *Aldwater Toll Bridge*: **guards the path** to Veyne Hall.
  - *Millhaven Road*: optional. A Hound patrol, and a prize-fighter who's
    betting he can beat all of them.
- **Town:** Millhaven (market town; the first world-map shop).
- **Side quest:** *The Company Ledger*, Tamsin's. Unlocked after the Toll Bridge
  is cleared, with Tamsin recruited and alive. She finds the crown paymaster
  who pocketed the Red Company's wages, and has to decide what the money is
  worth.
- **Arcs advanced:** the lead and Dace, Hollis (his secret is out), Tamsin.
- **Recruit:** **Gil Parrow** (Brawler), the "Champion of Six Fairs", a
  Yakuza-style oddball with an absurd code of honour. Recruited in the
  *Millhaven Road* skirmish.

### Chapter 5: Salt and Silver

- **Goal:** find someone who can stop the war. The Brennish envoy in Saltmere,
  **Ragna Holt**, needs proof that the "Brennish raids" are staged, and Joss is
  a Hound willing to testify.
- **Conflict:** the Saltmere League is neutral and for sale. A magistrate sells
  the envoy's safe house to the Hounds. Aske's secret comes out: the raiders
  he sold news to answer to the envoy's network.
- **Turn:** the envoy gets Joss's testimony, but the League expels everyone
  involved. Ragna: both armies are massing at the Ashfields. "If you want to
  stop this war, you stop it there, or it's the Ashfields again."
- **Story battle: "The Envoy's House".** Holding a harbour-front house at night
  against Hounds coming from the docks and the rooftops, for a number of
  turns.
- **Fixed skirmishes:**
  - *The Salt Road*: **guards the path** from the heartland to Saltmere.
  - *Smugglers' Cove*: optional, with loot.
- **Town:** Saltmere (the biggest shop in Act 1).
- **Side quests:**
  - *Letters Home*, Aske's. Unlocked after the story battle, with Aske
    recruited and alive. A refugee boat carrying his letters and silver home
    has been seized by League privateers.
  - *The Captain's Oath*, Hollis's. Unlocked after Chapter 4's story battle,
    with Hollis alive. At his wife's grave near Veyne Hall, Hollis tells the
    lead everything: the purse, the list, and why he burned them.
- **Arcs advanced:** Aske (his secret), Hollis (confession), the lead and
  Hollis (mending).
- **Recruit:** **Hedda Ravn** (Raider), Ragna's Brennish bodyguard, loaned to
  the lead "to make sure you get there".

### Chapter 6: The Ashfields

- **Goal:** reach the Ashfields before the two armies clash, and put the
  envoy's proof in front of the Brennish commander.
- **Conflict:** the plain is full of **echoes** (fire elementals: old vows
  still burning). The king's army has brought new Candles.
- **Turn:**
  - **Oriel Mast**, the crown battle-mage who gave the Candles the order three
    years ago, deserts to the party. He confesses that the prince rode into
    the field and burned on **the king's own order**.
  - Rue tells Hollis that Wren walked out of the fire alive.
  - The Brennish commander believes the proof, but the king's army attacks
    anyway. The war is on.
- **Story battle: "The Ashfields".** Crossing a burnt plain between two armies,
  with echoes and battle notes ("Echoes of Vael: weak to frost, drink fire").
  Objective: reach the Brennish lines (escape-style; the chapter ticket
  decides).
- **Fixed skirmishes:**
  - *The Burnt Mill*: **guards the path** to the plain. The first echoes.
  - *Brennish Outriders*: optional.
- **Town:** Cairnford (border town).
- **Side quests:** none new (Act 1's side quests stay open).
- **Arcs advanced:** Rue and Aske (she was a Candle; his brother burned
  there), Hollis (Wren lives), the king's secret (for the player).
- **Recruit:** **Oriel Mast** (Sorcerer, tier 2): a tired, guilty man who
  never wants to burn anything again, and has to.

### Chapter 7: Ossary

- **Goal:** find the surviving Candles, and Wren, in the Vigil's holy city.
- **Conflict:** the city is full of pilgrims and wardens, and Piers's old
  master is there.
- **Turn:** the Candles' dormitory under the city is empty. **Crane** is
  waiting. He calls death "a door", and tells Piers his report was
  "admirably thorough". The party learns that the Door was opened once before,
  in the East, and that the Jade Reach sealed it. Only the East knows how to
  shut it. Crane escapes.
- **Story battle: "The Undercroft".** Wardens in catacombs and narrow
  stairs. The boss is Crane's champion; Crane himself leaves.
- **Fixed skirmishes:**
  - *Pilgrims' Road*: **guards the path** into Ossary.
  - *The Ossuary Gate*: optional.
- **Town:** Ossary (relics and the Vigil's market).
- **Side quests:**
  - *The Other Cart*, Rue's. Unlocked after Chapter 6's story battle, with Rue
    alive. A Candle who escaped with her runs a hideout for runaways.
  - *Unwritten*, Piers's. Unlocked once Ossary is reached, with Piers alive.
    He brings the register's names back to their families.
- **Arcs advanced:** Piers (faces Crane), Rue (the children), Dace (seen
  briefly, shaken by Crane).
- **Recruit:** none (or the runaway Candle from *The Other Cart*, decided by
  that side quest's ticket).

### Chapter 8: The Last Watch (Act 1 finale)

- **Goal:** get out. The king has declared the lead a traitor and gone to war.
  Ragna has a Brennish ship waiting at Saltmere to take the party east, to the
  only people who know how to shut the Door.
- **Conflict:** Dace and the Hounds are on the docks.
- **Turn:** **Hollis dies.** He holds the gangway so the party can board.
  Dace orders the archers to stop, too late. Hollis dies between his son and
  the lead, telling Dace he's ashamed of him and loves him. The ship sails.
  Dace stands on the dock alone. This is the scripted loss Nick asked for
  (Expedition 33 at the end of Act 1).
- **Story battle: "Saltmere Docks".** Hold the dock for a number of turns while
  the ship loads, against Hounds from three streets. Dace is the boss and
  can't be killed; he retreats.
- **Fixed skirmishes:** none (it's the last push). **Town:** Saltmere (one last
  shop before leaving).
- **Arcs advanced:** Hollis (ends), Dace (his turn begins), the lead (the
  mentor is gone), everyone (the goodbye).
- **Classic permadeath:** if Hollis already fell, the scene is rewritten so
  that the lead tells Dace instead (the Chapter 8 script ticket writes both
  versions).
- **Recruits:** none. *Act 1 ends.*

## Act 2: The Far Shore (the Jade Reach): one paragraph

The party lands in the Jade Reach as foreigners and outlaws: a land of rival
sects who train breath instead of swearing vows, sword-saints and mountain
monasteries, and duels settled on rooftops. The Jade Reach remembers the night
the Door opened in its own history, and the sect that sealed it isn't eager to
teach a stranger how. The chapters are sect trials, duels and a tournament
(the wuxia encounters Nick asked for), with eastern recruits joining the army.
Dace follows with a company of Hounds, hunting the lead, and begins to doubt
what he serves. **The midpoint twist** lands here (options below). Rue swears
her fire vow anew; Aske, Tamsin, Piers and Oriel find what they're fighting
for. Act 2 ends with the lead learning how the Door can be shut, and at what
price, and sailing home with eastern allies.

## Act 3: The Door (the Old Kingdoms at war): one paragraph and the ending

Home is at war: Ardeval and Brennmark bleeding on a new map, with Harrowby
burned and Candles on every front, each death feeding the Door. The lead
rallies the people Act 1 met: Tamsin brings the Unpaid companies, Aske and
Ragna bring Brennmark, Piers brings the keepers who refuse the Door, and the
East sends its allies. Dace, broken by his father's death and by the midpoint
twist, confesses publicly to the night at the mill and the year before it, and
turns. The last battle is fought at the Door on the Ashfields.

**Ending:** a resolved, hard-fought victory, never a tragedy (Nick).
- **The Door:** Dace pays the Door's price himself, and dies, so Wren and the
  lead live (Nick: redeemable, "turns in the last act and pays for it").
- **The king:** faces what came back and lets go. The Door is shut.
- **The war:** Brennmark and Ardeval make a peace worth the name, and the
  Candles go home.
- **The lead:** what they do with the Veyne name (take it back, refuse it, or
  make it mean something) is the player's reply tone, never a branch.

Each companion gets an epilogue line. How paired endings work with unlimited
A-ranks is still open (`supports.md`).

## The midpoint twist: options for gate 2

Nick deferred it until the lead was known ("Same as 1"). It lands in Act 2,
around chapter 12.

- **A. The prince already came back** (like Expedition 33 or NieR).
  - **What it is:** the Ashfields' deaths paid for one soul, and something
    came back wearing Aurel's face. The king has hidden it in Varenhall for
    three years. It's hollow and hungry, and it's what's really pushing for
    war, to become whole.
  - **Pros:** it recontextualises everything, and the king becomes a victim.
  - **Cons:** the main villain changes halfway through.
- **B. Crane wants the god, not the boy** (Dark Souls lore).
  - **What it is:** opened fully, the Door returns Othe himself. Crane
    believes that ends death for everyone. The king is his key, and the
    prince is his proof.
  - **Pros:** the dead-god lore pays off, and the king can be redeemed.
  - **Cons:** the finale risks "stop the evil god". It has to stay a door,
    not a monster.
- **C. Wren is the Door** (Yakuza-personal).
  - **What it is:** Wren is alive. She's the bearer: every death under the
    Door Vow has passed through her, and opening the Door will kill her. The
    king's plan to bring back his son is killing Dace's sister, and Dace has
    been serving it.
  - **Pros:** it fuses the rival plot with the main plot, and sets up Dace's
    turn. It pays off Rue's secret and gives Hollis's death its sting.
  - **Cons:** it's smaller in scope, and Wren must have real choices, not be a
    prize.

**Claude's recommendation:** C as the midpoint twist, and B as the Act 3
reveal (Crane's real aim). A personal twist in the middle, an epic one at the
end.
