# Rue

Id: `heretic` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 1. She carries the truth about
  Dace and about Wren.
- **Class:** **Mage** (fire; swords in tiers 1–2).
- **Talent:** Mag.
- **Age:** 19.

## At a glance

A gifted girl from a Thornmarch charcoal-burners' family, taken twice:

- at 15 she was in Vosse's cart on the night at the mill, and escaped in the
  chaos after Vosse died;
- a year later she was caught by Vigil hunters, sworn to fire and made a
  **Candle**.

At 16 she burned at the Ashfields with the other Candles and deserted in the
smoke. There she swore a fire vow of her own, which makes her a heretic. Her
hands are scarred from that day. This autumn she took shelter with Keeper
Piers at Harrowby as his "cook" (she can't cook).

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to find the other children (the Candles who survived) and burn the
  people who took them.
- **Need:** to stop being only the thing that was done to her, and to trust
  people enough to let them help.
- **Flaw:** fire first. Suspicious, reckless and rude, and she pushes people
  away before they can hand her over.
- **Secret:** she knows two things nobody else in the party does:
  - she saw **Dace** at the mill that night, helping load children into
    Vosse's cart;
  - she saw **Wren Marr alive** after the Ashfields fire, being led away by
    keepers.

  She keeps both to herself because she thinks the lead, Dace's old friend,
  was in on it.
- **Pressure:** she's a heretic and a deserter. Every keeper and Hound would
  take her back, and Piers's report told them where she was.

## Arc

- **Start:** a hunted girl who trusts nobody and burns first.
- **End:** she swears her fire vow again, in front of the party, in new words:
  "I will burn only what I must." In the finale she chooses to shield rather
  than burn. She finds the children, and some of them come home.

| Where | What changes |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Ch1 | Recognises the Veyne seal on Harl's orders. Tells the lead, coldly, that she's seen it before. |
| Ch3 | Tells the lead what Dace did at the mill, and hears Piers confess the report. Both relationships crack before they can heal. |
| Ch6 | At the Ashfields, tells Hollis that Wren was alive after the fire. Aske learns she was a Candle. |
| Ch7 | Side quest "The Other Cart" (a Candle who escaped with her). |
| Acts 2–3 | Swears her vow anew; finds the children. |

## Voice notes

- Short, rude and funny. Answers questions with questions. Swears by the dead
  gods irreverently ("Vael's teeth"). Talks about food constantly; she was
  hungry for years.
- Precise and cold when she talks about the Candles; no jokes there, ever.
- Calls the lead "heir" (sarcastic) until she trusts them. Calls Piers "Keeper"
  with a sneer, then "Piers" by the end.
- **Won't say:** "please". She'll say "give it here" instead.

**Sample lines**

- "I'm not a cook, Keeper. I'm a girl who can light the oven without a match.
  Different thing."
- "I've seen that seal before, heir. On a cart. At night. With children in it."
- "Your friend lifted me in by the collar. He said 'sorry, little one.' He was
  very polite about it."

## Portrait brief

- **Silhouette:** small, sharp and hunched, with a too-big soldier's coat
  (stolen) and the collar turned up to the ears, and a sword hilt at the hip.
- **Hair:** black, chopped short and uneven with a knife, sticking out.
- **Face:** 19, thin and angular, with dark eyes that squint at everyone. A
  faint pale burn mark climbs from her jaw to her ear on the right side. Light
  shading.
- **Hands (if in frame):** wrapped in stained linen to the knuckles.
- **Clothing:** an oversized dark red-brown Ardevali greatcoat with the badges
  torn off, and a grey scarf.
- **Colours:** rust red-brown, charcoal black, grey, and ember orange for any
  fire.
- **Expressions:** `neutral`, `happy` (a sharp grin), `angry`, `sad`,
  `surprised`, plus `sneer`.
