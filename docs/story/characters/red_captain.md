# "Red" Harl Coster

Id: `red_captain` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** the **Chapter 1 boss** (named, with a portrait, `chapter-1.md`).
  Captain of the Red Company, one of the Unpaid. He dies in Chapter 1.
- **Class:** **Brigand** (enemy-only; axes). Stats and loadout are set by 0803.
  He's the only enemy in Chapter 1 who uses an active skill
  (`progression.md`: only bosses do).
- **Age:** 46.

## At a glance

Harl was a good sergeant and a better captain in the war, and he brought most
of his company home alive. The crown never paid them. He's spent three years
watching his men starve, drink and die of cold, and he stopped caring whose
coin he took. He taught Tamsin to ride. Last winter he burned the Coldwell farm
for a lord. This week he took a contract under the Veyne seal to kill "the
exile, the old man, and the girl with the burned hands" at Harrowby.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to die with his men paid, fed and not ashamed. He settles for
  paid.
- **Need:** to remember he used to be someone Tamsin would follow. (He never
  gets there, which is the point: he's what Tamsin could become.)
- **Flaw:** bitterness that turned into "everyone's price is only a number".
- **Secret:** he knows who hired him. It was a Hound, with a letter and a
  purse, and the seal was Veyne's. He'll say it as he dies, to hurt.
- **Pressure:** his men haven't eaten properly since summer.

## Arc

- **Start:** a veteran captain who sold the last of his honour.
- **End:** dies still owed, in the hamlet he came to burn. His death quote
  hands the plot to the lead.

## Voice notes

- Gravelly, blunt and tired. Soldier's jokes gone sour. Calls everyone by
  their price or rank.
- **Won't say:** that he's ashamed. He says "the war did this".
