# Keeper Piers

Id: `keeper` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 1. The party's healer, and the
  Vigil seen from inside.
- **Class:** **Cleric** (healing vows; gauntlets).
- **Talent:** Res.
- **Age:** 34.

## At a glance

The Vigil's keeper of Harrowby for six years: anxious, fussy, genuinely kind,
and physically unable to lie. He believes in the vows and in the Vigil,
because the Vigil taught him to heal. He tested Wren Marr and sent her name to
the Vigil three years ago. This autumn he sheltered Rue, a gifted girl with
burned hands, and **dutifully reported her**, asking the Vigil to help her
gently. That report is why the killers came.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to be a good keeper: heal the sick, keep his vows, and keep the
  Vigil's peace.
- **Need:** to learn that faith isn't obedience. He can keep his vows to the
  dead gods while breaking with the living Vigil.
- **Flaw:** obedience, and conflict avoidance. He assumes the institution
  means well because *he* does.
- **Secret:** he wrote both reports, Wren's three years ago and Rue's this
  autumn. He learns what the second one caused in Chapter 1, and confesses
  it to Rue in Chapter 3.
- **Pressure:** leaving Harrowby with the lead makes him an apostate as well as
  an outlaw.

## Arc

- **Start:** an obedient keeper who trusts the Vigil completely.
- **End:** a keeper without a Vigil. In Act 3 he stands against Crane, his
  superior, in front of other keepers, and some follow him. Faith kept,
  institution refused.

| Where | What changes |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Ch1 | Learns Harl came for "the girl with burned hands", and realises his own letter sent them. |
| Ch3 | Takes the Greywater register; confesses to Rue. Breaks with the Vigil. |
| Ch7 | Faces Crane in Ossary; side quest "Unwritten": he returns the names in the register to the families. |
| Act 3 | Leads the keepers who refuse the Door. |

## Voice notes

- Long, polite sentences that interrupt themselves; qualifiers ("I think,
  possibly, with respect..."). Nervous humour under pressure: he babbles.
- Quotes vows and small prayers to Ama, then adds a practical comment ("Ama
  keep you. Also, keep pressure on that.").
- Can't lie. When he tries, he fails out loud ("I was not... that is, I have
  no... yes. Yes, I did.").
- **Won't say:** anything cruel, even to Crane. His defiance is polite, which
  makes it land harder.

**Sample lines**

- "Ama keep you. Also, and I say this as a keeper, please keep pressure on
  that, it's not supposed to be on the outside."
- "I wrote to them. About you. I asked them to be gentle. I thought... I
  thought that was a thing you could ask."
- "With respect, Master Crane, and I do mean that, I have a great deal of
  respect: no."

## Portrait brief

- **Silhouette:** round-shouldered and soft, with the Vigil keeper's tall
  grey hood folded back into a thick collar, and a small lantern charm on a
  cord.
- **Hair:** thinning light brown, with a neat tonsure-like part and a
  close-shaved beard.
- **Face:** 34, round and kind, with worried eyebrows and ink stains on his
  fingers when his hand is in frame. Light-to-medium shading.
- **Clothing:** grey Vigil robes with a white stole embroidered with a
  simple open-hand sign (Ama's), and sleeves rolled up for work.
- **Colours:** dove grey, white, candle yellow (charm) and ink black.
- **Expressions:** `neutral`, `happy`, `angry` (rare: a frown more than a
  scowl), `sad`, `surprised`, plus `flustered` (wide eyes, mouth open
  mid-word).
