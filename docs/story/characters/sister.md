# Wren Marr

Id: `sister` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** NPC in Act 1: missing, and believed dead. She's the heart of the
  Marr family's story and a key to the Door plot. Her part in Acts 2–3
  depends on the midpoint twist (gate 2).
- **Class:** none in Act 1. If she becomes playable later, a gifted class,
  decided by the Act 2/3 tickets.
- **Age:** 16 (12 on the night at the mill, 13 at the Ashfields).

## At a glance

Hollis's daughter and Dace's little sister. She is gifted, and Dace kept her
name off Vosse's lists until Vosse found out and took her anyway. Dace
killed Vosse to get her out of that cart. A year later, while Dace was at
war, Keeper Piers tested her at Harrowby, as the law required, and the Vigil
took her to be a Candle. She was on the Ashfields. Her family was told she
burned there. She didn't: Rue saw keepers lead her away alive after the fire.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** to go home. (She remembers Harrowby, her father's moustache, and
  the lead teaching her to skip stones.)
- **Need:** to choose her own vow, not the one sworn for her.
- **Flaw:** she does what she's told. It kept her alive.
- **Secret:** where she is and what she's for. It's decided at gate 2, and
  it's one of the midpoint twist options.
- **Pressure:** everyone who loves her has been told she's dead.

## Arc

- **Start:** gone.
- **End:** home, in the victory, and alive. Nick's ending is a hard-fought
  victory, and bringing Wren home is part of what's won. Dace dies making that
  possible.

## Voice notes (for when she appears)

- Quiet, formal and trained: she speaks like a Candle, in the Vigil's
  phrasing. Flashes of a Harrowby kid underneath (slang, jokes about her
  father's moustache).
- **Won't say:** "I want". She says "I was told".

**Sample lines**

- "I was told my family was informed. I was told that was a kindness."
- "Da's moustache. Does he still have the moustache? Tell me he still has the
  moustache."
- "I was sworn to fire at thirteen. I'd like to swear something else now."

## Portrait brief

- **Silhouette:** slight and upright, in a plain grey Candle's smock with a
  high neck, and a thin iron vow-ring around the throat like a collar.
- **Hair:** Hollis's brown, long, and braided tightly down the back in the
  Vigil's style.
- **Face:** 16, pale from being indoors, with the Marr jaw, and Dace's eyes

- **Clothing:** a grey smock with a small embroidered flame at the collar (the

- **Colours:** grey, iron, pale skin and ember orange (the flame).
