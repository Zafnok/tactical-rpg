# Tamsin Rook

Id: `sergeant` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** playable companion from Chapter 1. The party's swagger, and the
  voice of the Unpaid.
- **Class:** **Rider** (mounted; swords and spears).
- **Talent:** Spd.
- **Age:** 27.

## At a glance

A cavalry sergeant of the Red Company, one of the Unpaid, who quit Red Harl's
band last winter. She guards Harrowby for bread, ale and a dry stable, and
keeps a battered ledger of every coin the crown owes her and her dead
friends. The ledger is her joke, her grudge and her grief, all in one book.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** the back pay and the land grant she was promised in the war:
  **4,212 crowns**, and forty acres of river bottom. "I'll take it in cash, in
  land, or out of somebody's hide."
- **Need:** to find something worth fighting for that nobody can pay her for.
- **Flaw:** everything is a transaction. She jokes her way out of any moment
  that might cost her something to feel.
- **Secret:** last winter she rode with Harl when the Red Company burned a
  farm at Coldwell for a lord's coin. She held the horses and didn't stop it.
  She left the next day and has told nobody why.
- **Pressure:** the Unpaid are her people. Every brigand the party kills could
  be someone she drank with.

## Arc

- **Start:** a mercenary who says she's only here for the pay.
- **End:** she tears the ledger up (Act 3), because what she's fighting for
  isn't owed to her; she's giving it.

| Where | What changes |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Ch1 | She kills her old captain, Harl, and pretends it's just a job. |
| Ch4 | Side quest "The Company Ledger": she finds the paymaster who pocketed the Red Company's wages, and has to decide what the money's worth. |
| Act 1 | Admits the Coldwell farm (supports with Hollis and the lead). |
| Act 3 | Rallies the Unpaid companies to the lead's side in the war's last battles. Pays off her Unpaid thread. |

## Voice notes

- Quick and loud, with long sentences that turn into a punchline. Ledger
  jokes: "Put it on the crown's tab."
- Uses soldier slang ("hayburner", "the Unpaid", "pay-day" for a battle she
  expects to win).
- Calls people by nicknames: "Your Former Grace", "Keeper", "Sparks" for Rue,
  "Brennish" for Aske, "Captain" for Hollis.
- **Won't say:** "I'm scared" or "I'm sorry". She says "that'll cost us"
  instead.

**Sample lines**

- "Harl's whole sorry company, on foot, before breakfast. I'm adding
  breakfast to the ledger."
- "Your Former Grace, if we're committing treason, I'd like it noted I'm doing
  it for free. Well. For bread."
- "I held the horses. That's all. I held the horses and I watched it burn."

## Portrait brief

  over one shoulder and a riding cap pushed back. Unmistakable against the
  others' heavier outlines.
- **Hair:** dark auburn, cropped short at the sides, and a messy top that
  escapes the cap.
- **Face:** 27, sunburned, with a wide crooked grin, a chipped front tooth and
  freckles. Light shading.
- **Clothing:** a patched red cavalry jacket (the Red Company's colour),

  pouch.
- **Colours:** faded brick red, brass, auburn and dark brown leather.
- **Expressions:** `neutral`, `happy` (a big grin), `angry`, `sad`,
  `surprised`, plus `smug` (one eyebrow up, half smile).
