# Dace Marr

Id: `rival` · [Name registry](../names.md)

## Role and class

- **Role:** the rival and main secondary villain (Nick, gate 1: the person the
  lead took the fall for, who turns out not to be who the lead thought and
  "become[s] somehow one of the more important antagonists"). **Redeemable:**
  he turns in the last act and pays for it (Nick: "A").
- **Class:** enemy **Shadowblade** in Act 1 (swords and gauntlets); later
  Nightblade. In the last chapters of Act 3 he fights on the player's side for
  a short time (as a guest or playable unit; decided by the Act 3 chapter
  tickets).
- **Talent:** Spd.
- **Age:** 22.

## At a glance

- **Who he was:** Hollis's son, raised beside the lead at Veyne Hall. He was
  quick, funny, charming, and never allowed to forget he was the servant's
  boy. He is also **gifted**, and has hidden it all his life. Vosse found out,
  and made him a deal: point out other gifted children, or be registered
  himself. At 17 Dace was pointing them out, and taking Vosse's silver for it
  ("the Vigil schools them; they eat better than we do"). He stamped the
  cart's road passes with the Veyne seal from Lord Veyne's study.
- **The night at the mill:** he brought two children to the cart as usual and
  found his own sister Wren in it. Vosse had found out Dace kept her name off
  the lists. Dace killed Vosse, and the other children ran. When the lead
  arrived, Dace told the truth, but only part of it: "He took Wren."
- **After:** the lead's confession saved him. He went to war and rose by
  being brilliant and ruthless. Now he's a hero, captain of the king's
  Hounds, and holds the Veyne lands and title.
- **Wren:** taken by the Vigil after he left. He was told she died at the
  Ashfields. The king has promised to bring her back through the Door. He
  serves the Door for her.

## Want, need, flaw, secret

- **Want:** Wren back, and to *be somebody*: the name, the hall, the respect
  he was born outside of.
- **Need:** to take responsibility, which means saying out loud what he did,
  and paying for it himself, instead of letting someone else pay.
- **Flaw:** self-justification. He's always the hero of his own story ("I did
  what fed us. You had a house; I had a sister."). His charm is how he gets
  others to carry his weight.
- **Secret:**
  - The year of selling children to Vosse, which only Rue (a witness) and
    Hollis (who suspects) know.
  - **He's gifted**, and has never sworn a vow. Nobody alive knows (Vosse
    did). That's what lets him take Wren's place as the Door's bearer at the
    end: the first and only vow he ever swears.
  - In the present: he sent **both** the warning letter and Harl's kill
    order. Crane ordered the witnesses silenced; Dace obeyed, and warned the
    lead first because he couldn't not.
- **Pressure:** the king holds Wren's return over him. Crane holds his past
  over him.

## Arc

- **Start:** a charming, successful man carrying a life built on someone
  else's sacrifice.
- **End:** he confesses publicly (Act 3), turns on the king and Crane at the
  Door, and dies paying for it: he takes Wren's place as the Door's bearer so
  Wren lives and the Door closes (Nick: "turns in the last act and pays for
  it").

## Voice notes

- Warm, quick and funny, the charm of a born talker. Says the lead's name a
  lot ("Veyne. Veyne. Listen to me."). Mimics voices; tells stories about
  their childhood to disarm people.
- When cornered, the charm goes cold and precise: short clauses, no jokes.
- He always has a reason, and always says it first.
- **Won't say:** "I'm sorry" until Act 3, and when he finally does, it's the
  whole confession.

**Sample lines**

- "Veyne. You look terrible. Exile suits you about as well as the title did,
  and you wore that like a borrowed coat."
- "I did what fed us. You had a house. I had a sister and a father who made
  soldier's pay. Don't you dare call it choosing."
- "You gave me my life. I've been paying interest on it every day since. Do
  you know what that's like? No. You don't. You've never owed anyone."

## Portrait brief

- **Silhouette:** tall and elegant, with a Hound's black half-cloak pinned at
  one shoulder and a high collar. Sharp and expensive: the lead's silhouette,
  if the lead had won.
- **Hair:** Hollis's dark brown gone darker, swept back neatly, with one loose
  lock.
- **Face:** 22, handsome in an easy way, with Hollis's jaw and a quick smile
  that doesn't reach the eyes. A thin scar at the corner of the mouth. Light
  shading.
- **Clothing:** black Hound leathers with silver studs, and a **Veyne-blue**
  sash across the chest bearing the full Veyne crest, the crest the lead cut
  off. A silver signet ring.
- **Colours:** black, silver, and Veyne blue (the same blue as the lead's
  coat, on purpose).
- **Expressions:** `neutral`, `happy` (the charming smile), `angry`, `sad`,
  `surprised`, plus `cold` (no smile, eyes flat).
